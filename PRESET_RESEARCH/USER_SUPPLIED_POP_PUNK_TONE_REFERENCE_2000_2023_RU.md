## 1. Genre-wide 2000s starting formula

Approximate values on a 0–10 scale:
- Gain: 5.5–6.5
- Bass: 5
- Mid: 5.5–6.5
- Treble: 7
- Presence: 6
- Reverb: 0 on rhythm guitars; short plate only as a possible lead starting point
- Pick: 0.60–0.73 mm

Principle: bright, fast, moderate gain; power chords remain intelligible at speed; moderate low end prevents chord smear.
