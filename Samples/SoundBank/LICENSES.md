## Tier 2 — royalty-free but NOT redistributable as isolated samples

These sources are intentionally excluded from PPGU_SampleLibrary.zip unless the rights holder supplies a separate written redistribution / sample-library embedding grant.

- Sample Focus: standard license allows royalty-free use inside new creative works, but prohibits redistributing sounds in isolation or including them in a sample library / sample pack. No files included.
- Looperman: loop usage is subject to site terms; standalone redistribution as a sample pack is not permitted. No files included.
- Splice / Mike Naran Pop Punk Riffs: royalty-free for new recordings/creative works, but prohibits isolated sublicensing and redistribution in a new sample pack or competing product. No files included.

## Tier 3 — CC-BY

No CC-BY samples are included in this build. If added later, each file must have its own recorded attribution in this document (creator, exact asset title/URL, license version, required credit text, source download date) and its license text must ship in the package.

## Excluded / pending review

- Freesound Ax_Grinder pack 14939: excluded until the individual recording pages, per-file license, author and download permissions have been verified. Pack membership alone does not prove CC0.
- Freesound CC0 search results: excluded from this build until every selected recording has verified CC0 metadata and authorized original download.
- Selekt Audio guitar MIDI: excluded until per-file certificate, author and exact license are recorded.
- ProducerSpace / Signature Sounds: no files included in this build because the licensing/redistribution terms for each individual guitar asset were not verified.
- Any pirated, leaked, ripped or unlicensed commercial library: prohibited and not included.

## Engineering note

The archive contains source multisamples and mappings, not a claim that every articulation or MIDI groove is already implemented in the plugin. Octave, palm-mute, release-noise and loop categories are populated only where real source material exists; missing performance material must be recorded or sourced under a compatible license, never synthesized by relabeling unrelated samples.
